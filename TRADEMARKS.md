# GSF project name, logos and official releases

**Scope:** This is a project identity and anti-impersonation guideline,
**not** a change to the GPL code license. The terms in
[LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) continue to allow use, study, modification
and redistribution of the covered source, including commercial use.

- **Official source:** https://github.com/wavessevaw/GSF
- **Official releases:** only releases and assets explicitly published
  by the maintainers of that repository and verified by their
  published provenance/checksums, if any.
- **Independent forks:** may modify, distribute and commercially use
  GPL-covered software in compliance with the license. They should
  clearly identify themselves as independent and distinguish their
  modified builds from official releases.
- **Attribution and compatibility:** factual references such as
  "based on GSF" or "compatible with GSF" are welcome when truthful;
  do not imply maintainers tested, certified, sponsored or endorsed a
  fork when they have not.
- **Impersonation:** do not falsely label a modified or unsafe build as
  an official maintainer release or use confusing presentation to
  misrepresent its source.

The GPL **does not itself convey ownership or registration of a
trademark**. This document does not assert that "GSF" is a registered
trademark or grant the repository administrator exclusive ownership of
a generic acronym. The availability, distinctiveness and enforceability
of any project name/logo rights depend on applicable law, prior users,
actual ownership and potentially registration. A formal trademark
application should follow a clearance search and legal review.

Nothing in these guidelines forbids fair, truthful or otherwise lawful
use of the project name, imposes extra conditions on code licensed under
GPL-3.0-or-later, or asserts GSF owns Valve/Steam Deck, AMD, Vulkan,
MAKO or other third-party marks.
